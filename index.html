Make This Summer Count

Summer in Iowa City offers plenty of ways to explore, relax, and spend time with friends. Whether you are staying near campus, taking summer classes, or visiting for a few weeks, you can find something fun to do without planning a big trip.

From downtown coffee shops and local bookstores to scenic walking trails and community events, this guide helps college students plan a memorable summer on any budget.

Your Summer, Your Way

You do not need a packed schedule to enjoy Iowa City. Try one new place each week, plan a picnic with classmates, explore a neighborhood you have not visited, or set aside an afternoon to read outdoors. A few small plans can make a summer in town feel like an adventure.

Before heading out, check current opening hours, event details, transportation options, and any admission costs. Activities and schedules may change throughout the season.
