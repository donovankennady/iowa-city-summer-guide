Get Outside This Summer

Longer days and warmer weather make summer a great time to explore Iowa City outdoors. Whether you enjoy a peaceful walk, an afternoon with friends, or a little adventure on two wheels, there are plenty of ways to build outdoor time into your routine.

1. Take a Scenic Walk

Choose a public trail or walking route and enjoy the scenery at your own pace. Invite a friend, listen to a podcast, or leave your headphones behind and take in the sounds around you. Check the route and conditions before leaving.

2. Plan a Park Picnic

Pack sandwiches, fruit, and a cold drink for a relaxed outdoor meal. Bring a blanket, a book, or a deck of cards. Remember to clean up after yourself and follow any posted park rules.

3. Go for a Bike Ride

Explore a suitable local bike route and enjoy a different view of the area. Wear a helmet, bring water, check your tires and brakes, and choose a route that matches your comfort level. Follow road signs and trail etiquette.

4. Watch the Sunset

Find a safe, permitted public spot with an open view and plan a low-key evening outdoors. Bring a light jacket, take a few photos, and enjoy a break from screens before heading home.

5. Look for Outdoor Community Events

Check local event calendars for seasonal markets, public performances, outdoor art activities, and neighborhood gatherings. Confirm dates, locations, admission details, and weather updates before making plans.

6. Try a Nature Photo Challenge

Take your phone on a walk and capture five things: a flower, an interesting tree, a reflection, a pattern, and something that makes you smile. Compare pictures with friends or create a personal summer photo album.

Before You Head Outside

  • Bring drinking water and use sun protection.
  • Check the weather forecast and any severe-weather alerts.
  • Wear comfortable shoes and clothing suited to the activity.
  • Check park hours, trail conditions, and posted rules.
  • Carry your phone and let someone know your plans for longer outings.

Build Your Summer Routine

Try scheduling one outdoor activity each week. Rotate between a walk, picnic, bike ride, and community event to keep your routine interesting. You can also combine outdoor plans with our suggestions for free student activities and other Iowa City summer outings.
